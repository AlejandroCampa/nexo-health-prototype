The process

Three steps between you and a real prescription.

Every patient goes through the same clinical path — no shortcuts, no self-selected dosing.

01

Intake & history

A structured health history and lab upload, reviewed before your visit so time with your physician is spent on decisions, not paperwork.

02

Physician visit

A licensed physician evaluates your goals — weight, hormonal health, recovery — and determines what's clinically appropriate.

03

Prescription & delivery

Approved prescriptions route directly to our 503A pharmacy partner and ship to your door with dosing instructions from your physician.

What to expect

Give it three months.

Dose increases gradually, on a physician-set schedule — not all at once. Most patients need at least 3 months of consistent treatment before seeing their full results. This isn't a quick-fix product, and we'd rather tell you that upfront than have you stop right before it starts working.

Month 1

Starting dose

Treatment begins at a low starting dose while your body adjusts. Early changes are often subtle — this month is about tolerance, not results.

Month 2

Titrating up

Your physician increases your dose based on how you're responding. This is usually when patients start noticing real changes in appetite and weight.

Month 3+

Full effect

By now most patients are at or near a therapeutic dose. This is where consistent, ongoing progress happens — and why membership is monthly, not one-and-done.

About Nuvi Clinic

Physician-led metabolic and longevity medicine, built for Puerto Rico.

Nuvi Clinic is a telehealth practice offering physician-supervised weight management and longevity care. We were founded on a simple frustration: getting real, well-supervised metabolic care usually means long waits, rushed visits, and pricing nobody can explain.

How we're different

Every prescription is reviewed by a licensed physician. Not a questionnaire, not an algorithm. You complete a health history, then speak with a nurse practitioner under physician supervision before anything is prescribed. If a medication isn't appropriate for you, we say so.

One flat price, whatever your dose. Your medication costs the same every month — it doesn't go up when your provider increases your dose. Most clinics charge more as you titrate up. We don't. Your membership is separate and never bundled into a number you can't break down.

Dispensed by a licensed compounding pharmacy. Your prescription is prepared by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy and shipped directly to you.

Local. We're based in Puerto Rico and currently serve Puerto Rico patients. Expanding to additional jurisdictions requires physician licensure in each one, so we do it deliberately rather than all at once.

For providers

Working with Nuvi Clinic as a clinician or referring provider.

Referring a patient

If you have a patient who would benefit from supervised metabolic or weight management care, you can refer them directly. They'll complete our intake, be interviewed by our care team, and every prescribing decision is made by our supervising physician — we'll keep you informed if your patient asks us to.

Joining our care team

We work with nurse practitioners and physicians licensed in the jurisdictions we serve. Providers must hold an active, unrestricted license, a current DEA registration where applicable, and an NPI. All prescribing follows documented protocols with physician oversight — we don't operate a rubber-stamp model, and we're not a fit for clinicians looking for one.

Pharmacy and fulfillment

Prescriptions are fulfilled through a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy partner and shipped directly to patients. Ordering is handled by our clinical staff; providers are not asked to manage logistics.

To discuss working together, contact info@nuviclinic.com.

Effective date: [DATE TO BE SET ON PUBLICATION]

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

Our commitment

Nuvi Clinic is required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information (PHI), to give you this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow the terms of the notice currently in effect. We are also required to notify you if a breach of your unsecured PHI occurs.

How we may use and disclose your health information without your authorization

Treatment. To provide, coordinate, and manage your care. For example, we share your prescription and relevant history with our compounding pharmacy partner so your medication can be prepared and shipped, and among our own providers so they can make safe clinical decisions.

Payment. To bill and collect payment for services. For example, we process your membership and medication charges through our payment processor. We do not send clinical details to our payment processor.

Health care operations. To run the practice — including quality review, evaluating provider performance, training, and business management.

As required by law. When federal, state, or Puerto Rico law requires it.

Public health and safety. To prevent or reduce a serious threat to health or safety; to report suspected abuse, neglect, or domestic violence; to report adverse events or product problems to the FDA; and for public health activities such as disease reporting.

Health oversight, judicial, and law enforcement. To health oversight agencies for audits and investigations; in response to a court order, subpoena, or other lawful process; and to law enforcement in the limited circumstances the law allows.

Others involved in your care. If you agree, or if you are not present and we judge it to be in your best interest, we may share information relevant to your care with a family member or friend involved in it.

Uses and disclosures that always require your written authorization

  • Most uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes.
  • Uses and disclosures for marketing purposes.
  • Any sale of your protected health information.
  • Most other uses and disclosures not described in this notice.

If you give us written authorization, you may revoke it at any time in writing. Revoking it will not affect disclosures we already made while it was in effect.

Your rights

Get a copy of your record. You can ask to see or get an electronic or paper copy of your medical record. We will provide it within 30 days, and may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee.

Ask us to correct your record. You can ask us to correct information you believe is incorrect or incomplete. We may say no, but we will tell you why in writing within 60 days.

Request confidential communications. You can ask us to contact you in a specific way, or at a different address. We will say yes to all reasonable requests.

Ask us to limit what we use or share. You can ask us not to use or share certain information. We are not required to agree, and may say no if it would affect your care. However: if you pay for a service in full out of pocket and ask us not to share that information with your health insurer, we must agree unless a law requires us to share it.

Get a list of disclosures. You can request an accounting of certain disclosures we made in the six years before your request.

Get a copy of this notice. You can ask for a paper copy at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

Choose someone to act for you. If you have given someone medical power of attorney or they are your legal guardian, that person can exercise these rights on your behalf.

File a complaint. If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you can file a complaint with us using the contact details below, or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at 200 Independence Avenue SW, Washington DC 20201; 1-877-696-6775; or hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint.
